# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in ATF to be a straightforward, harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be direct and be kind. Technical disagreement is welcome; contempt is not.
- Critique the code, the design, or the argument — not the person.
- Assume the other person is competent and acting in good faith, and ask before concluding
  otherwise.
- Accept that maintainers may decline a change. A "no" with a reason is a complete answer.
- Respect that reviewers and maintainers are often volunteering their time.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, intimidation, or discriminatory language, public or private.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Personal or political attacks, insults, or demeaning comments.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or persistently reopening settled decisions.
- Disclosing a security vulnerability publicly before a coordinated disclosure, contrary to
  [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages,
code and documentation content — and when representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to <conduct@abillify.me>. Reports are handled confidentially.
Include what happened, where, when, and any links or screenshots.

Maintainers will respond within 5 working days. Responses are proportionate and may include a
private warning, a request for a public apology, temporary restrictions on interaction, or a
permanent ban from project spaces. Maintainers who do not uphold this code in good faith may
themselves face consequences.

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other
contributions that do not align with this code, and will explain moderation decisions when
asked.
