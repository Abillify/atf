## 1. Parties, execution, and covered Contributions

The "Contributor" is the individual or legal entity identified in the applicable signature block
and, for a Corporate Agreement, the covered contributor identities listed in its schedule. A
"Contribution" is copyrightable or patentable material intentionally submitted by or on behalf
of the Contributor for inclusion in ATF after this Agreement has been validly executed, including
source code, object code, documentation, prompts, tests, designs, and other authored material.

Before making a Contribution, the Contributor must separately execute this Agreement as either
an Individual Agreement or a Corporate Agreement using the applicable signature and schedule
below. Electronic signatures and counterparts may be used where legally effective. A Corporate
Agreement must identify the contributing legal entity, the signatory's authority, and each
employee, contractor, service account, or other contributor identity it covers; the Contributor
must keep that schedule current.

Every commit must then contain this trailer:

```text
ATF-Contributor-Assignment-Accepted: 1.0, Full Name <name@example.com>
```

The trailer documents that the identified contributor is covered by a previously executed
Agreement of the stated version. It does not itself execute this Agreement and does not replace
any signature, written-form, or other formality required by applicable law. Abillify may reject
or defer a Contribution until it verifies the executed Agreement, identity, authority, and
coverage.
